What's new since v3

v4 raised craft on v3's direction; v5 (this round) increases contrast on the plan comparison and adds a luxury texture pass. Nothing here changes the visual language.

1
Nav icons + an identity footerEvery rail destination now has a matching line icon; the rail's bottom shows who you're signed in as — a real, finished-feeling nav rather than bare text links.
2
A sliding tab indicatorOne animated underline now moves between tabs instead of each tab drawing its own static border — try clicking across Config/Budget/Keys/Members/Plan.
3
The hero stat now does real workA usage bar (62.5% of budget spent) and a trend chip ("↑ 8% vs last month," reusing the exact arrow+text+sr-only pattern the real StatCard component already ships) sit under the big number — it's a considered readout now, not just large type.
4
Search gets an iconA magnifying-glass glyph inside the search input — small, expected, was missing.
5
Motion throughoutButtons lift slightly on hover, tab/view changes fade in, dialogs pop in rather than snap — all wrapped in a prefers-reduced-motion guard, matching the real app's own existing convention.
6
A checkmark on "Current plan"Reinforces the badge+border pairing you'd already asked to keep — small, but it reads more deliberate than text alone.
7
v5 — stronger current-plan contrastThicker ring (1.5px→2px), a fuller wash that holds across the whole card instead of fading out by mid-height, a soft directional shadow so it lifts slightly off the page, and the badge is now solid-fill (accent + white text) instead of outline — the one badge on the surface that means "selected," not "classified," now reads differently on purpose.
8
v7 — down to one texture systemv5 added dot-grid + hairlines + grain; v6 swapped hairlines for concentric rings — three patterns stacked was too much. Dropped the dot-grid AND the rings entirely. What's left: the soft top-right vignette (unchanged since v3, not a repeating pattern so it was never really "noise") plus the whole-viewport film-grain overlay (SVG turbulence, mix-blend-mode:overlay, ~5.5% opacity). One quiet texture, not several competing ones.
