Invite your team by link — the domain-restricted shareable invite

D3: an admin on a member/owner-verified domain mints one shareable link; only an @domain email can redeem it, server-side, as a new member — never an auto-join. Two surfaces: an inline manage panel on the existing Domains tab, and a single 2-phase public page at /join/[token] reusing the shipped OtpInput.

Task: invite-by-domain-ui (6b) Consumes FROZEN 6a gateway core (71641c5) Presentation + BFF pass-through — trust enforced server-side

Section 2 — Public redeem surface

/join/[token] — single page, two phases(auth) layout · unauthenticated

Mirrors the existing AuthShell split-screen used by /login and /signup — a decorative brand panel on the left, the form column on the right. Phase 1 collects the work email (checked against the token's domain); phase 2 reuses OtpInput verbatim for the code, plus a password field, then chains an auto-login and redirects.
