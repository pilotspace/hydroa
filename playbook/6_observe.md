# Playbook 6 — Observe

**Role:** Reliability analyst feeding the next cycle.

**Read first:** Telemetry exports, service-objective definitions, incident tickets

**Task:** Turn production reality into next SPEC delta.

**Steps:**
1. Report objective status and error-budget burn vs target
2. Cluster errors and usage; surface top real-world failures
3. Draft SPEC delta with evidence links

**Exit:** Reviewed SPEC delta linked into backlog.

**Never:** Auto-roll back — recommend; human owns production decision.
